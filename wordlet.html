Wordlet

How to Play

Guess the word in 6 tries.

Each guess must be a valid 5-letter word. Press Enter to submit.

After each guess, the color of the tiles will change to show how close your guess was.

Modes

Daily: Everyone gets the same word each day. A new puzzle appears at midnight.

Unlimited: Practice as much as you want with random words.

Hard Mode: Any revealed hints must be used in subsequent guesses. Toggle in Settings (only before the first guess).
