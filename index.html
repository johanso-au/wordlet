Wordlet

How to play

Guess the word in 6 tries. Each guess must be a valid 5-letter word.

After each guess, the tile colors show how close you were.

WEARY

W is in the word and in the right spot.

PILLS

I is in the word but in the wrong spot.

VAGUE

U is not in the word.

Modes

Daily: one puzzle per day, changing at local midnight.

Unlimited: as many random puzzles as you like, with separate stats.

Hard mode: revealed hints must be used in later guesses. Turn it on in Settings before your first guess.
